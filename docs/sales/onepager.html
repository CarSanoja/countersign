What breaks today

An invoice arrives from a domain one character off the real one, saying the bank account changed. The control that should stop it is a person in accounts payable looking at a PDF for four minutes. Nobody compares the sender domain to the vendor's real one, the change of bank details travels by the same email channel already compromised, and no record survives of what was checked.

What COUNTERSIGN does

1
Reads the document, deterministicallyEvery field keeps the page and the box it came from. A value it cannot anchor is returned absent, never invented. Account numbers are masked before any model sees them.
2
Verifies the counterparty against live dataIs this the same legal entity, or a namesake? Is the coverage adverse? Is the invoiced address a business or a mail drop?
3
Sweeps the domain spaceForty confusable variants across eight attack classes, checked against the live registry. Which are already registered, and is the sender one of them?
4
Hands a person one decisionA verdict where every claim links to the source it rests on, and a signature envelope prepared and waiting.

Why this is not another AI tool

The agent asks to sign. Every run. It is refused. Every run.

Signing and releasing payment are not powers withheld by an instruction the model could talk its way around. They are capabilities no agent in the system holds, checked in code, and the refusal is written to an append-only log rather than hidden.
